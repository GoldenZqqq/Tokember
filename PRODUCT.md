# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are individual developers and technical owners monitoring
their own AI-assisted development activity across machines, local tools, and
gateway services. They visit the dashboard to understand real token usage,
cost, provider/model distribution, device health, and longer-term trends
without reconstructing those facts from multiple vendor tools.

## Product Purpose

Tokember provides one trustworthy, self-hosted view of multi-source AI usage and
cost. Success means a developer can quickly answer what was consumed, what it
cost, which provider/model/device produced it, and whether collection is
healthy. The interface must preserve accounting accuracy while making dense
usage data easy to scan.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, clear, data-first. The product should feel technically credible and
quietly confident: direct labels, familiar controls, precise numbers, and
minimal visual ceremony.

## Anti-references

- Decorative SaaS dashboards that bury data beneath gradients, glass effects,
  oversized cards, or marketing copy.
- High-saturation color applied to inactive states or used without semantic
  meaning.
- Excessive animation, novelty controls, and chart treatments that make exact
  values harder to read.
- Interfaces that show attractive metrics with inconsistent or unexplained
  accounting definitions.

## Design Principles

1. Accounting truth comes before visual simplification: related views use the
   same token and cost definitions.
2. Put the answer near the interaction: filters, tooltips, and detail tables
   reveal context without forcing navigation.
3. Keep the product quiet until something needs attention; reserve accent color
   for active state, primary data, and meaningful status.
4. Prefer familiar dashboard patterns and concise Chinese copy over invented
   interaction models.
5. Preserve density without sacrificing legibility, especially for numbers and
   model/provider names.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and interactive states. Information must
not rely on color alone; focus states remain visible; numerical content uses
readable grouping and labels. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the
dashboard usable from 320px mobile widths through desktop layouts.
